Diagram Notes

Two-Level Disjoint Specialization: The relationships marked with "is a (d)" indicate a disjoint subset. A vehicle is split into a 2-wheeler, 4-wheeler, OR 6-wheeler. Those categories are then disjointly specialized further (e.g., a 4-wheeler is either a Car or a Jeep).
M:N Resolution: The many-to-many relationship between Mechanics and Spare Parts (Uses) is resolved conceptually through the AVAILABILITY bridge table.
Work Orders, Maintenance & Fuel: Added near Service, Company, and Vehicle to accurately reflect the operational logic in your updated physical diagrams.
